# AI ROI Calculator for SMEs

Find where AI pays off for a small or medium business, estimate the return, and plan the implementation — in English, Traditional Chinese, or Japanese.

**Live demo:** https://maokuanghsin.github.io/ai-roi-calculator-for-smes-/

## What it does

- **Business profile** — 21 industries, team size, monthly revenue range, main pain point, and which AI the business already uses (none / Microsoft 365 Copilot / Google Gemini / ChatGPT or Claude / several)
- **Process picker** — customer service replies, quoting / invoicing, inventory / ordering, marketing content, meeting notes & follow-ups, sales follow-up, data entry / reporting; enter hours per week and hourly cost for each
- **ROI results** — per process: hours saved per month, money saved per month, estimated AI tool cost, net monthly saving, and payback period; use cases ranked by ROI so the highest-return work comes first, with a live ROI dial and score
- **Already-have-AI adjustment** — if the business already uses Copilot, Gemini or another AI, generic-task savings are reduced so results are not double-counted; the adjustment is shown transparently in the assumptions table
- **Totals** — total monthly saving, total annual saving, and payback time
- **Implementation roadmap** — quick win (week 1–2), first agent (month 1), scale (month 2–3), with concrete next steps
- **Honesty panel** — these are estimates based on your own inputs, not a guarantee; AI costs vary by vendor and volume, and savings depend on adoption

Two one-tap sample businesses are included — a small e-commerce shop and a small accounting / professional services firm — so you can see a full report right away.

## Privacy

Runs **100% in the browser**. Nothing you enter is uploaded or stored. The report can be copied in full or downloaded as a PDF (`ai-roi-report.pdf`).

## How to use

Open the live demo above, or open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no server.
