An honest note on these numbers

  • These are estimates built from your own inputs — not a guarantee of savings.
  • AI tool costs vary by vendor, plan and usage volume. Treat the ranges here as planning figures, and get real quotes before you commit.
  • Savings depend on adoption: a tool nobody uses saves nothing. Training and habit change are part of the real cost.
  • “Hours saved” only becomes money if the freed time goes to revenue work, or lets you avoid a future hire.
  • Start with one process, measure for a month, then decide — that is what the roadmap below the results is for.
